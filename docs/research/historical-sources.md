# Historical National Points Race sources

## Source policy

The original Extemp Central NPR page is the archive index. Winner and runner-up facts come from Extemp Central's published ten-year summary. Complete standings remain attributed to the linked original workbook or Google Sheet. The new archive does not infer missing rows.
